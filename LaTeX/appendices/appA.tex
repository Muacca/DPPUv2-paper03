\section{Conventions and Computational Pipeline}
\label{app:A}

This appendix collects the notation conventions and the computational pipeline
for reproducing the results of this paper.

\subsection{Notation Conventions}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Spatial tensor indices: $i,j=1,2,3$.
  \item Spin-2 basis label: $A=1,\ldots,5$.
  \item Spin-1 triplet label (consistent with the scripts): $k=0,1,2$.
  \item Volume factor: $\Vol = 2\pi^2 L r^3$.
  \item Effective potential:
        $\Veff = -\!\left(\tfrac{R}{2\kappa^2}+\theta_\NY N\right)\Vol$.
  \item Pontryagin density:
        $P = \innerprod{R}{\star R}
           = \tfrac{1}{2}\,\varepsilon^{abcd} R_{abef} R_{cd}{}^{ef}$.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Where the Mode Dictionary Is Fixed}

The mode dictionary is presented in a distributed manner.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \S\ref{sec:setup} provides the overview from raw geometric variables to
        the final basis.
  \item \S\ref{sec:spin1} establishes the physical/auxiliary classification of
        the spin-1 sector.
  \item \S\ref{sec:eft} fixes the definitive dictionary adopted in this paper.
  \item Appendix~\ref{app:H:numerical} collects the numerical anchors
        corresponding to that dictionary.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Computational Pipeline}

The numerical and symbolic computations of this paper are conceptually organised
in the following order.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Specify the homogeneous frame and the structure constants $C^i{}_{jk}$.
  \item Compute the curvature, Ricci scalar, Nieh--Yan density, and Pontryagin
        density from the Einstein--Cartan connection.
  \item Assemble $\Veff$ and evaluate the Hessian and generalised eigenvalues at
        the isotropic point and at deformed points.
  \item Organise the full shear basis $T_A$, spin-1 physical/auxiliary basis
        $(X,Y)$, and torsion modes into the final basis.
  \item As additional consistency checks, independently verify the cross-Hessian,
        homogeneous consistency, and invariant generation with separate scripts.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Sanity Checks}

All conclusions used in the main text have passed at least the following sanity
checks.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Reproduction of raw spin-1 quadratic coefficients
        $(\mu_\omega^2, \mu_\delta^2, m_{\omega\delta})$.
  \item All 21 cross-Hessians are zero.
  \item Homogeneous Maurer--Cartan consistency.
  \item Exact check of the invariant basis $\{I_2, I_3, I_4, W_2\}$.
\end{enumerate}
These constitute reproducibility information supporting the main propositions of
this paper.
